2. Information collected

Game progress on your device

The Game stores progress, collected fish, coins, tutorial status, preferences, and gameplay counters locally on your device. It does not require a name, email address, phone number, or account registration to play. Some gameplay information is also sent as analytics events, as described below.

Information collected through integrated services

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and the services involved, integrated SDKs may collect:

  • Identifiers: Android Advertising ID where available, app set or installation identifiers, and SDK-generated identifiers.
  • Device and connection information: IP address, device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, language, time zone, network information, and approximate country or region inferred from an IP address. The Game does not request access to GPS or precise device location.
  • Gameplay and app activity: sessions, active play time, run and fishing-spot progress, catches, tutorial steps, equipment and perk choices, in-game currency actions, and interaction with ads or a review invitation.
  • Advertising and attribution information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completion, ad revenue, install-referrer information, and campaign attribution.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, stack traces, app errors, and device or performance information relevant to diagnosing a problem.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

  2. Tell us that your request concerns Reel Deal: Fishing Fun. If available, include the app version, approximate dates of play, and an advertising or installation identifier that may help locate your records. Do not post identifiers publicly.
